# Solar for every roof

A short pitch on bringing affordable rooftop solar to small businesses across the region.

## The problem

- Energy bills keep rising every year
- Rooftop solar looks complicated and expensive
- Installers quote slowly and inconsistently
- Financing is hard to compare
- Maintenance is an afterthought
- Small businesses are left without a clear path
- Many roofs sit unused

## Our approach

We survey the roof, size the system and handle permits, installation and monitoring under one fixed-price contract. Customers see their savings from the first month and keep one contact person for the whole life of the system. Our crews are trained in-house and every installation is checked by a second engineer before it is switched on. This keeps quality high while keeping costs predictable for the customer. We also publish production figures so that every customer can compare their system with the forecast.
